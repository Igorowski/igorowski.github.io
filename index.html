AMERICAN MOTORCYCLIST CONFEDERATION

Founded in 1924, the American Motorcyclist Confederation (AMC) is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting and protecting the interests of motorcyclists across the United States. With a rich history spanning nearly a century, the AMC has been at the forefront of advocating for motorcycle rights, safety, and education.

The AMC serves as a unifying force for motorcyclists, providing a platform for riders to connect, share their passion for motorcycles, and work together to address common issues. The organization offers a wide range of services and benefits to its members, including access to exclusive events, discounts on motorcycle gear and insurance, and resources for motorcycle safety and education.

One of the key missions of the AMC is to advocate for motorcycle rights and safety. The organization actively engages in legislative efforts at the local, state, and federal levels to ensure that motorcyclists have a voice in shaping policies that affect their rights and safety on the road. The AMC also promotes motorcycle safety through educational programs, rider training courses, and public awareness campaigns.

In addition to its advocacy work, the AMC organizes a variety of events and activities for its members. These include motorcycle rallies, charity rides, and social gatherings that foster camaraderie among riders. The AMC also hosts an annual convention where members can come together to celebrate their shared passion for motorcycles and discuss important issues facing the motorcycling community.

Overall, the American Motorcyclist Confederation is a vital organization that plays a crucial role in supporting and advocating for motorcyclists across the United States. Through its dedication to promoting motorcycle rights, safety, and education, the AMC continues to be a driving force in the motorcycling community, ensuring that riders can enjoy their passion while staying safe on the road.
